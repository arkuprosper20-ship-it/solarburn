Planet positions follow real J2000 Keplerian elements — eccentric orbits, perihelion orientation, axial tilts and day lengths included (orbital inclinations are flattened into the map plane). Planet sizes stay exaggerated in both distance modes so worlds remain visible. Good for orientation, not for ephemeris work.
