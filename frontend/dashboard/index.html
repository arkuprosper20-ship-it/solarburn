What the pipeline does

  1. Fuse raw 1-second SoLEXS soft X-ray and HEL1OS hard X-ray streams across 259,000+ coincident timestamps.
  2. Nowcast flare onsets with a strictly history-only rolling baseline, so nothing depends on future samples.
  3. Forecast activity up to 30 minutes ahead with pre-flare rise, QPP variance and Neupert-effect indicators.
  4. Present live alert levels, ionospheric impact and CME arrival estimates for operators.
